### What's New

- Timeline view for track history.
- Starred tracks in station track history.
- Recording sidecars are now stored next to the recordings, and older recordings can be imported through folder access.
- Recording folders now show the station logo.
- Recording file names use the schedule name for scheduled recordings (#230).
- New setting for the time format used in recording file names (#227).
- Loading indicator on the recordings tab.
- The search bar's hidden state is remembered across app restarts.
- Volume boost is now opt-in, with a safety warning before it's turned on.

### Improvements & Fixes

- Fixed playlist stream URLs not resolving.
- Fixed player controls becoming unresponsive when the player was expanded with the keyboard open (#221).
- Fixed playback stopping when opening the app after playback was started from a cold start.
- In-app volume now persists across station changes until playback stops.
- Fixed stations overriding in library.
